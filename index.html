What we do

Engineering services built around your project

Six core services, each handled by qualified engineers with the documentation, drawings and reports that banks, insurers and approving authorities expect.

Insurance Surveyor

Independent survey and loss assessment for insurance claims, with detailed, defensible reports covering cause, extent and quantum of loss.

Property Valuation

Technical valuation of land, buildings and plant for bank lending, collateral and transactions, prepared for financial institution requirements.

Municipal Drawing

Building drawings prepared to municipal bylaws and the building code, ready for submission and approval.

Design
