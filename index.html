What we do

Five services, one standard of precision

From the first drawing to the final handover, we cover the full life of your project.

Property Valuation

Careful valuation of land, buildings, vehicles and other assets, based on a site visit and set out in a clear report.

  • Building details
  • Land area and use
  • Vehicles and other assets
  • Valuation report

Engineering Design and Procurement Support

Design, cost estimates and bills of quantities for projects of many kinds, with help buying the right materials and equipment.

  • Cost estimation and BoQ
  • Detail engineering and drawings
  • Bid document preparation
  • Procurement support as per prevailing laws

Loss Assessment for Insurance Survey

On-site survey of damage, repair costing and a clear report that supports an insurance claim.

  • Site inspection and photographs
  • Damage and repair BoQ
  • Site analysis
  • Survey report

Construction and Installation Supervision

On-site quality control, checking the work against the drawings from the first day to handover.

  • Construction supervision
  • Installation supervision
  • Drawing and site checks
  • Final billing

MEP, Architectural and HVAC Design

Building design for a wide range of projects, from architectural layouts to mechanical, electrical, plumbing and HVAC systems.

  • MEP design
  • Architectural design
  • HVAC design
  • Drawings and details

Supply and installation of industrial machinery, certified components, materials and hardware, with servicing and custom fabrication when you need it.
