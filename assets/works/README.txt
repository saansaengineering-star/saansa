The "Projects we have delivered" carousel is filled from js/works-data.js.
To add a project, copy its photo into assets/photos/ and add one entry there.

Tips
  - Wide photos (3:2 or 16:9) fit best. You can set a "position" for each photo
    in js/works-data.js to keep the subject in frame.
  - Keep each file under about 400 KB and 1600 px wide so the page loads quickly.
  - Use your own photos or photos you have the right to publish.
